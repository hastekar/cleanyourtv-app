# Store listing (English)

**Name (30):** Lumo Match: Fair Match-3

**Short description (80):** A match-3 puzzle where every level is proven winnable. No ads, no purchases.

**Subtitle – App Store (30):** The fair match puzzle

**Keywords – App Store (100):** match,match3,puzzle,gems,tiles,offline,no ads,fair,brain,casual

**Full description:**

Lumo Match is a match-3 puzzle that plays with you, not against you.

• PROVEN LEVELS — None of the 60 levels ships until a computer has verified it can be won without a single booster.
• FIXED, FAIR DEALS — The order tiles fall in is fixed per level. It never quietly gets harder because of how long you play or how often you win.
• NO PURCHASES, NO ADS — Extra moves are not for sale. Boosters are only earned by playing.
• NO LIVES, NO WAITING — Play as much as you like, whenever you like.
• OPEN HELP — Lose the same level several times in a row and you get a few extra moves; the game tells you so, and you can turn it off.
• OFFLINE — No internet needed. No account. No data collected.
• FOR EVERYONE — Every tile differs by shape as well as colour, so it is colour-blind friendly.

Match 4 for a line tile. Make an L or a T for a bomb. Match 5 for the rainbow tile. Break the ice, smash the crates, clear the goals.
